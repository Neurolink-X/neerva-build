# Academy knowledge lifecycle

## Architecture
Handbook (long-form stage field guides) → related lessons → optional Q&A → calculators and project tools. Stable chapter and lesson IDs, canonical URLs, sitemap, compact directory search and bounded browser progress. Keep core science separate from regulations, products and prices. No claims of ten-year guaranteed relevance.

## Editorial contract
Each chapter stores revision, publication date, review due date, explicit review state, scope, authority directory references, principles, example, risks, checklist and linked lessons/tools. All current handbook content is editorial draft for learning; not professionally reviewed. Review due is a scheduling marker, not a promise that the review will occur. A qualified reviewer must verify exact sources, editions, local applicability and translations before marking reviewed; store review evidence in the PR and update dates/revision. Never silently change stable IDs or repurpose checklist entries; increase chapter revision when changing a checklist. Old local records remain isolated by revision. Add new stage articles in the registry and validate cross-links in tests.

## Update cadence
Six-monthly editorial review, immediate review when a relevant standard or rule changes, and separate current verification for local rules, product specs or prices. No invented regulatory or engineering sign-off. Use authority directories as starting points, then cite the exact applicable edition and relevant clauses in reviewed content. Keep market figures timestamped and outside evergreen chapters. A future admin publishing workflow should have roles, draft/review/publish, audit history, rollback, translation checks and scheduled review alerts; this implementation is Git-reviewed content, not a live CMS.

## Engagement and portability
Learning progress, practical checklists, real scenarios, local calculators, design alternatives, exports and print guides support useful repeat visits. No streak pressure or paid dependency. Inspiration exports human-readable design briefs; Room Lab supports versioned JSON export and bounded additive import without overwriting saved alternatives. Existing designs, board sharing and local saves remain. High-resolution assets and richer render models remain separate work.

## Material atlas
Fifteen material records and twenty glossary terms are separated from the stage curriculum, with stable anchors, chapter/lesson links, example arithmetic and source scope. BIS and BEE authority directories are reference starting points (BIS concrete standards directory, BEE residential building energy code), not verified clause-level professional review. Atlas editorial revision and review date must be updated with reviewed changes.

## Site Practice Lab
Twelve authored simulated situations cover all twelve handbook stages. Separate situational judgement, action steps, evidence requests and conversation scripts from theory. Quotes and delivery tools accept user-entered amounts, explicitly distinguish taxes/charges and rejected/missing stock, and do not execute purchasing. Treat scenarios as editorial educational material; qualified site review and real project evidence remain necessary. Keep numerical examples illustrative and stable case IDs.

## Scope boundary: learning vs project execution
Academy prioritises workmanship, construction science and site decisions. Optional written-price comparison and delivery reconciliation are in the private My Project workspace, not the learning lab. Eight practical workmanship records use original schematic illustrations where useful, clear warning signs, tools and professional escalation. Illustrations are not site photographs or formal acceptance criteria. Professional review remains pending.
